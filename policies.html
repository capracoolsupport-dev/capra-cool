Policies & Terms.

Clear terms, transparent shipping, and straightforward returns. Everything you need to know about shopping with Capra Cool.

SECTION 01

Shipping & Delivery Policy

All orders placed on Capra Cool are dispatched from our central fulfillment facility within 24 to 48 hours of order confirmation (excluding Sundays and national holidays).

  • Free Shipping: All prepaid and COD orders totaling ₹999 or more qualify for 100% FREE express shipping.
  • Standard Shipping Fee: For orders below ₹999, a flat shipping fee of ₹99 is charged across India.
  • Estimated Delivery Times:
    • Metro Cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata): 3–5 business days.
    • Tier 2 & 3 Cities: 4–6 business days.
    • North-East, J&K, Himachal Pradesh, and remote regional locations: 5–7 business days.
  • Order Tracking: As soon as your order leaves our warehouse, an SMS and WhatsApp dispatch message containing your live tracking AWB link will be sent to the contact number provided at checkout.

SECTION 02

Returns & Refund Policy

We want you to be completely confident in every piece you wear. We provide a 7-day return window beginning from the date your package is marked as delivered by the courier partner.

  • Eligibility Criteria:
    • Items must be unused, unwashed, and free of stains, scents, or animal hair.
    • All original garment tags, sew-in labels, and branded zip polybags must remain intact.
  • Return Process:
    • To initiate a return, contact our concierge at care@capracool.com with your Order ID and photos of the item.
    • We will arrange a reverse pickup from your original delivery address within 48–72 hours via our logistics partners (Bluedart / Delhivery / Xpressbees).
  • Refund Turnaround: Once the returned item is inspected at our hub (typically within 48 hours of receipt), refunds are processed within 3–5 working days to the original payment source (bank account, UPI, or credit card). For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are issued via direct UPI transfer or NEFT.

SECTION 03

Size Exchanges

Ordered a size that feels slightly too boxy or snug? We are happy to exchange your piece for an alternative size free of charge, subject to inventory availability.

Our courier partner will pick up the current size and deliver the replacement size simultaneously (doorstep exchange) in serviceable pin codes, or dispatch the replacement immediately upon return scan.

SECTION 04

Privacy & Data Protection

CAPRA COOL complies with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and modern data security standards. We collect only the information necessary to fulfill your order and communicate relevant updates:

  • Information We Collect: Name, delivery address, contact telephone/WhatsApp number, email address, and IP address for fraud prevention.
  • Payment Security: We do not collect or store credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or net banking passwords. Online payment options will be shown only when a verified payment gateway is active.
  • No Third-Party Selling: We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertising brokers. Data is shared strictly with delivery couriers and SMS/WhatsApp API gateways to communicate dispatch status.
  • Data Deletion Requests: You may request the permanent deletion of your customer profile and address data at any time by emailing care@capracool.com.
