01 / SHIPPING

Dispatch & delivery

  • Standard orders are normally dispatched from Himachal Pradesh within 24–48 hours after confirmation.
  • Typical delivery is 3–5 business days for metro cities and 4–6 business days for regional destinations. Remote pin codes may take longer.
  • Shipping is complimentary when the merchandise subtotal is ₹999 or more. A flat ₹99 shipping charge applies below that threshold.
  • Any major delay or stock issue should be communicated using the email or phone details supplied with the order.
Delivery estimates are targets, not guarantees. Weather, remote-route serviceability, public holidays, or courier disruptions can extend delivery time.

02 / RETURNS & EXCHANGES

Seven-day request window

  • Return or exchange requests for standard, non-custom products should be raised within seven days of delivery.
  • Items should be unworn, unwashed, undamaged, and returned with original tags and packaging where reasonably possible.
  • For a sizing issue, contact care@capracool.com with the order reference and requested replacement size.
  • Items showing wear, washing, alteration, fragrance, stains, or damage after delivery may not qualify.
Custom-produced campus merchandise is made to specification and is handled separately under the custom-order terms below.

03 / CUSTOM CAMPUS ORDERS

Artwork before production

  • Custom T-shirts, hoodies, and tracksuits move to production only after garment, colour, artwork placement, quantity, timeline, and pricing are confirmed.
  • Live website mockups are visual guides. Final print dimensions and colours are confirmed during artwork review.
  • The customer is responsible for providing artwork or marks they are authorised to reproduce.
  • Because customised merchandise is produced to specification, cancellation, return, and exchange terms may differ from standard retail products and should be confirmed on the quote before payment or production.

04 / PRIVACY

Your order details

When you order, we collect your name, email, phone number, delivery address and selected items to process the order, coordinate dispatch and provide support. Order information is stored using Supabase.

Checkout currently uses Cash on Delivery. This form does not ask for card details or payment passwords. For questions about the personal information you provide, contact care@capracool.com.

05 / SUPPORT

Talk to a person

For sizing, order changes, custom merchandise, delivery questions, or exchange requests, contact care@capracool.com. Support hours shown on the storefront are Monday–Saturday, 10 AM–7 PM IST.
