Talk to us ↗
How do I order a design?
Open a design, choose your size and add it to your bag. You can add a name or department print for ₹51 per garment. Checkout uses Cash on Delivery.
Can I add my name or order for a group?
Name or department printing is available for ₹51 per garment. Add the exact text when choosing your product. For group changes, use the enquiry form above. We confirm artwork before production.
Where can I find the size guide?
Ask for the measurement guide for your selected garment when enquiring. We’ll confirm the appropriate chart before you choose a size.
What about delivery and exchanges?
Delivery dates and any terms for customised pieces are confirmed with your quote. Read our delivery and custom-order information.
