HELP & SIZING

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our fits, ordering, and delivery across India.

What are the available sizes and how do they fit?

Our launch edit ranges from S to XXL. Our T-shirts feature an everyday comfortable drop-shoulder cut, while our hoodies and tracksuits are structured for high-altitude warmth and city layering. Click "ℹ" on any piece to view exact chest, length, and sleeve measurements.

How much is shipping and how fast does it arrive?

Shipping is completely FREE for all orders above ₹999. For orders below ₹999, a flat shipping fee of ₹99 applies. Orders are dispatched from Himachal Pradesh within 24–48 hours and arrive in 3–5 business days in metro cities, and 4–6 days in regional areas.

What is your return & exchange policy?

We provide a 7-day hassle-free return and exchange window from the delivery date. Items must be unworn and in original condition with tags intact. Sizing exchanges are completely free.

Can I request assisted ordering?

Yes. Use the assisted order option at checkout and your order request will be prepared for our concierge team by email. We will confirm availability, payment, and dispatch details before processing.

Where is Capra Cool located?

Capra Cool is headquartered in Mandi, Himachal Pradesh (31.7086° N, 76.9419° E), surrounded by the Shivalik and Dhauladhar ranges of the Himalayas.
