HELP / BUYING GUIDE

Questions, answered clearly.

How do I choose my size?

Open any product and select “Size guide.” Tees are listed by chest, length, and shoulder; hoodies use relaxed chest measurements; tracksuits include waist and garment lengths. If you prefer a looser fit, move one size up.

How long does delivery take?

Orders generally dispatch within 24–48 hours. Delivery typically takes 3–5 business days to metro cities and 4–6 business days to regional destinations. Remote locations may take longer.

What is the exchange policy?

Exchange or return requests can be raised within seven days of delivery for unworn items with original tags intact. Customised and bulk-produced merchandise may have different terms because it is made to specification.

How do custom campus orders work?

Build the live mockup, submit your preferred garment, colour, placement, quantity, and notes, and our team will reply with artwork confirmation, production timing, and a quote before production begins.

Can I order for a club, fest, department, or alumni group?

Yes. The Campus Atelier is intended for societies, fests, departments, teams, student bodies, alumni groups, and other institutional communities.

Where is CAPRA COOL based?

CAPRA COOL is based in Mandi, Himachal Pradesh, with a design language shaped by Himalayan weather, campus life, and everyday movement.
